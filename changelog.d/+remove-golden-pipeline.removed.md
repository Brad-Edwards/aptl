The golden-disk build, qualification, sealing and distribution pipeline is removed. A seat boots a published image, so there is nothing to bake per release: `aptl appliance` and its build, acquire, assemble, bundle, qualify, seal and split-distribution commands are gone, along with the signed release manifest, the candidate trust path, the participant qualification attestation, the chunked release transport and its resumable installer, and `scripts/appliance/` apart from the two scripts that still publish the project's container images. The release workflow loses its five self-hosted jobs and now completes entirely on hosted runners. The appliance boundary is unaffected: the policy, the gate, host exposure checks, the negative reachability probe and guest readiness observation all remain, sourced from the image the seat boots rather than from a release directory.
