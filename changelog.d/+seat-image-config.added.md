A seat image now describes itself. The resources its VM needs and the appliance boundary policy its guest launches under travel with the image rather than coming from a separately built release manifest, so the disk that boots and the contract describing how to boot it cannot drift apart. The boundary policy remains the existing platform model validated by its own rules, so loopback-only publication, the Docker authority and the containment contract are enforced exactly as before; an image asking for absurd resources, or publishing nothing a participant can reach, is refused instead of launched.
